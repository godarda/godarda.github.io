Exponents
# e raised to the power of 0
julia> exp(0)
1.0

# e raised to the power of 0 minus 1
julia> expm1(0)
0.0

# Exponential function e^x
julia> exp(1)
2.718281828459045

# e raised to the power of 1 minus 1
julia> expm1(1)
1.7182818284590453

# e raised to the power of 2
julia> exp(2)
7.38905609893065

# e raised to the power of 2 minus 1
julia> expm1(2)
6.38905609893065
Logarithms
# Natural logarithm (base e)
julia> log(0)
-Inf

# Natural logarithm (base e)
julia> log(2)
0.6931471805599453

# Base-10 logarithm
julia> log10(0)
-Inf

# Base-10 logarithm
julia> log10(2)
0.3010299956639812

# Base-2 logarithm
julia> log2(0)
-Inf

# Base-2 logarithm
julia> log2(2)
1.0

# Natural logarithm of 1 plus x
julia> log1p(0)
0.0
Square root and Cube root functions
# Square root
julia> sqrt(100)
10.0

# Cube root
julia> cbrt(27)
3.0

# Hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle
julia> hypot(3, 4)
5.0

# 2 raised to the power of 10
julia> 2^10
1024
