--- permalink: cg/ title: Computer Graphics description: Explore the world of Computer Graphics Algorithms and OpenGL, the cornerstone of visual computing. Learn the fundamentals of rendering, shading, and transformation algorithms, along with the power of OpenGL for creating dynamic and interactive graphics. introduction: - >- Computer graphics is the use of mathematics and software to create and display images. A program describes shapes and their positions, then calculates how those shapes should appear from a chosen viewpoint. The result is converted into colored pixels on a screen. Learning graphics connects familiar ideas such as coordinates and geometry with the steps a computer follows to draw a line, a shape, or a complex three-dimensional scene. - >- Graphics is used in video games, films, mapping, medical imaging, product design, and scientific simulations. A car designer may use a three-dimensional model to inspect a vehicle before building it, while a scientist may visualize data that is difficult to understand in a table. Graphics programmers use algorithms and libraries such as OpenGL (Open Graphics Library) to control how objects are transformed and drawn by a computer. - >- You will begin with points, vectors, coordinate systems, and transformations such as moving, rotating, and resizing an object. The lessons cover algorithms for drawing lines and circles, then introduce the OpenGL rendering pipeline, which is the sequence of steps that turns geometry into an image. You will work with vertex data, buffers, and shaders—small programs that calculate object positions and pixel colors—and connect them through model, view, and projection transforms. ---
